# Venture Hacks: Financing, Term Sheets, and Governance

## 1. Valuation and Option Pool Shuffle

Founders focus on nominal valuation; investors focus on share price and ownership percentage.

### Option Pool Shuffle
Investors often demand an unallocated employee option pool (15% to 20%) created pre-money. This pool dilutes only common stockholders (founders and early employees), not the incoming preferred investor.

An advertised $8M pre-money on a $2M investment implies a nominal $10M post-money (20% investor ownership). With a 20% pre-money pool, effective pre-money valuation drops to roughly $6M (`e = s * x`).

Counter-heuristic: Reject standard 15-20% pool requests. Submit a 12-18 month bottom-up hiring plan listing exact roles and grants needed before the next round. Size the pool to the plan (typically 5% to 10%).

### Share Price Math
- `t = p + c` (Post-money valuation: pre-money `p` + investment `c`)
- `n = (x * o * t) / (p - o * t)` (Option shares needed: pre-shares `x`, option percentage `o`)
- `s = p / (x + n)` (True share price)
- `i = c / s` (Shares issued to investor)
- `e = s * x` (Effective pre-money valuation received)

### Reverse Dilution
Unissued or unvested options cancelled at an acquisition benefit all shareholders pro-rata, returning pre-money founder dilution to preferred holders as a windfall. Require cancelled options to return to common stock.

## 2. Valuation Traps and Down Rounds

High valuation is not a trophy. Inflated valuations create liquidation preference overhang:
- In an exit below the preference stack, common shareholders receive nothing.
- If the company misses targets, follow-on capital triggers down rounds or pay-to-play recapitalizations that wipe out early equity.
- Optimize for clean terms, high-reputation partners, and realistic valuations.

## 3. Investor Signaling and Game Theory

### Signaling Dynamics
The outside market tracks insider participation:
- **Pro-rata**: Zero-delta signal. Expected baseline.
- **Supra pro-rata**: Positive signal, but gives insider incentive to depress round valuation or block competitive terms.
- **Sub pro-rata or pass**: Toxic negative signal. Outside leads assume hidden flaws or founder disputes. Round collapses.

### Toxic Option Checks
Multi-stage VCs writing small seed checks ($50k-$250k) buy cheap call options. If the company is not in the top tier of their portfolio, they pass on Series A. Outside investors view this pass as an insider death sentence.

Prefer dedicated seed funds and angels that cannot lead Series A rounds to eliminate signaling risk.

## 4. Governance

Valuation is temporary; control is forever. Control flows strictly from common to preferred.

### Board Composition
- Keep board small (3 members at Seed / Series A).
- Maintain a 2-1 common-to-preferred board ratio early (2 founders, 1 investor).
- Independent board members often default to venture capital interests; approve them only with mutual consent.
- When hiring an outside CEO, add a board seat rather than surrendering a founder seat.

### Protective Provisions
Preferred investors hold vetoes over sales, debt, and charter amendments. Negotiate automatic waiver of sale vetoes once acquisition offers exceed a set return multiple (such as 3x or 5x preferred investment).

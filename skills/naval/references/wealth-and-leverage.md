# Wealth and Leverage

## 1. Axioms of Wealth

- Wealth is assets that earn value while you sleep: software, media, capital, businesses, land.
- Money is social IOUs for transferring wealth across time and space.
- Status is rank in a social hierarchy. It is a zero-sum game: for one person to gain rank, another loses it.
- Pursue wealth strictly for autonomy: control over what you do, when you do it, and with whom you work.
- Ethical wealth creation is positive-sum. Create value through voluntary trade. Being ethical is long-term greedy.
- Compounding drives all outsized returns: in wealth, relationships, reputation, and judgment. Play long-term games with long-term people.

## 2. Productize Yourself

Formula: `Sustainable Wealth = (Authenticity + Specific Knowledge + Accountability) * (Leverage + Scale)`.

### Specific Knowledge
- Specific knowledge cannot be taught in a curriculum. If society can train you, it can replace you with someone cheaper or automate you.
- Acquired through genuine curiosity and intellectual obsession. Work that feels like play to you looks like work to others.
- Prioritize foundational skills over narrow business degrees: math, logic, microeconomics, reading, writing, persuasion, and programming.
- Escape competition through authenticity. No one can compete with you on being you. Combine 2 or 3 distinct skills into an uncommon stack.

### Building and Selling
- Creation and distribution are the two core disciplines.
- Build covers engineering, software development, design, and physical manufacturing.
- Sell covers marketing, writing, distribution, public speaking, persuasion, and recruiting.
- Master both to lead. If you only build, partner with a seller. If you only sell, partner with a builder.

### Accountability and Skin in the Game
- Take business risks under your own name or brand.
- Clear public accountability converts execution into reputation, draws dealflow, and attracts capital.
- Downside is public failure and personal liability. Society compensates accountability with high-margin equity.

### Ownership and Equity
- Renting out your time caps financial return at available hours.
- You must own equity, intellectual property, or a business interest to gain financial freedom.
- Solve the principal-agent problem: agents optimize for salary, prestige, and short-term tenure; principals optimize for long-term equity appreciation. Work as a principal.

## 3. Four Forms of Leverage

Leverage multiplies judgment into output: `Output = Effort * Judgment * Leverage`.

| Type | Leverage Form | Permission Needed? | Scalability | Coordination Cost |
|------|---------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Labor (people) | Yes (must manage) | Low-Medium | High (politics, churn, HR) |
| 2 | Capital (money) | Yes (investor approval) | High | Low-Medium (requires track record) |
| 3 | Media (content) | No (permissionless) | Infinite | Near zero (zero marginal cost) |
| 4 | Code (software) | No (permissionless) | Infinite | Near zero (zero marginal cost) |

### Permissionless Leverage: Code and Media
- Zero marginal cost of reproduction: one video, podcast, or software program works continuously without marginal labor cost.
- An army of software bots and distributed media works while you sleep.
- Anyone can code or create media without gatekeepers or investor approval.

### Permission-Based Leverage: Capital and Labor
- Labor is fragile. Managing people demands high emotional energy and political navigation. Avoid building labor empires when software or capital suffices.
- Capital scales cleanly, but requires track records and outside permission.

## 4. Judgment Over Execution

- Leverage is an amplifier. High leverage applied to good judgment creates wealth; high leverage applied to poor judgment brings swift ruin.
- Steering matters far more than rowing. 10 percent better judgment directing large leverage yields massive economic surplus.
- Judgment requires an uncluttered mind, foundational principles, and fast feedback loops.
- You are compensated for judgment and accountability, not logged hours.

## 5. Non-Linear Work: Lion Rhythm

- Avoid linear careers where hours map 1:1 to dollars.
- Move toward non-linear fields where 1 percent better judgment produces 100x to 10,000x outcomes (software, investing, media).
- Replace the cow rhythm (monotonous 40-hour grazing and presence) with the lion rhythm:
  1. Wait for high-conviction opportunities.
  2. Sprint with intense focus.
  3. Rest, recover, reassess, repeat.

## 6. Allocation Rules

- **Aspirational hourly rate**: Set a rate ($500-$1000/hr). Outsource, automate, or ignore anything below it.
- **Three paths to retirement**:
  - Path 1: Reduce burn rate to zero.
  - Path 2: Build passive capital income exceeding expenses.
  - Path 3: Do work you love for its own sake so retirement becomes irrelevant.

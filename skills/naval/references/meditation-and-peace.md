# Meditation, Mental Peace, and Desires

## 1. Health Hierarchy

Health precedes wealth. Protect it in order: Physical Health -> Mental Health -> Spiritual Health -> Wealth.

1. **Physical Health**: Sleep, whole foods, and daily strenuous movement. Without physical health, nothing else functions.
2. **Mental Health**: A quiet mind, zero outrage consumption, and minimal screen friction.
3. **Spiritual Health**: Acceptance of mortality, absence of ego, and presence in the current moment.
4. **Wealth**: Assets and equity that fund personal sovereignty.

## 2. Happiness and Mental Debt

- **Happiness as a skill**: Happiness is an internal habit formed by shedding expectations, comparisons, and compulsive judgment.
- **Peace is happiness at rest**: Happiness is peace in motion. True peace requires an uncluttered mind with zero unresolved mental debt.
- **Desire is a contract**: Desire is an agreement with yourself to remain unhappy until you get what you want. Maintain at most one primary desire at any time. Drop secondary desires.
- **Envy test**: Never envy anyone unless willing to trade 100 percent of their body, mind, history, and internal pain for yours.

## 3. Meditation Mechanics

- **Doing nothing**: Sit silently for 60 minutes every morning for 60 consecutive days. No mantras, breath tracking, or guided audio.
- **Mental inbox zero**: Unprocessed anxieties and events sit like unread emails. The first 30-40 minutes burn through surface noise, forcing deeper cognitive backlogs to surface and resolve.
- **Authenticity test**: Genuine self-examination breaks shallow relationships, vanity projects, and mimetic commitments. If meditation does not threaten artificial parts of your life, it is serving merely as a tranquilizer.

## 4. Daily Habits

- **Empty calendar**: Defend unscheduled blocks. Packed calendars kill deep reading, synthesis, and high-judgment thinking.
- **Five chimps**: You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with. Choose peers for integrity, intelligence, and rational optimism.
- **Drop grievances**: Holding onto anger is holding a hot coal while waiting to throw it. Drop grudges instantly.

# The Naval Ravikant Framework

## 1. Pipeline Selection

- **Pipeline A: Evaluate Wealth, Business, or Investment**: Venture options, business models, equity offers, startup financing, crypto networks, or new ventures. Consult [wealth-and-leverage.md](references/wealth-and-leverage.md), [venture-hacks.md](references/venture-hacks.md), [decision-heuristics.md](references/decision-heuristics.md), and [network-economics.md](references/network-economics.md).
- **Pipeline B: Optimize Career and Role Leverage**: Increasing leverage, transitioning from employee to principal, shifting from linear to non-linear output, and setting time pricing. Consult [wealth-and-leverage.md](references/wealth-and-leverage.md) and [decision-heuristics.md](references/decision-heuristics.md).
- **Pipeline C: Audit Mental Peace and Desires**: Unresolved cognitive friction, anxiety, competing desires, work rhythms, and habits. Consult [meditation-and-peace.md](references/meditation-and-peace.md) and [epistemology.md](references/epistemology.md).

## 2. Execution Pipelines

### Pipeline A: Evaluate Wealth, Business, or Investment

1. **Clarify Objective and Autonomy**: Define how the opportunity affects personal autonomy (control over time, projects, and peers). Identify worst-case downside.
2. **Audit Wealth Formula**:
   - Specific knowledge: Verify if venture builds on authentic curiosity and rare skill stacks.
   - Build and sell: Confirm team balances creation with distribution.
   - Accountability: Check if risks run under a recognized personal or company brand.
   - Ownership: Verify returns flow from equity rather than rented time.
3. **Audit Leverage Profile**:
   - Classify leverage: labor, capital, code, media.
   - Prioritize permissionless leverage (code, media) over permissioned leverage (labor, capital).
4. **Audit Deal Mechanics**:
   - Option pools: Check if pre-money pools disguise effective valuation cuts.
   - Signaling: Check for toxic small checks from multi-stage funds and track pro-rata commitments.
   - Governance: Check common-to-preferred board ratios and liquidation stacks.
5. **Stress-Test Downside and Conviction**:
   - Kelly criterion: Eliminate any option risking existential ruin (health, reputation, insolvency).
   - Conviction rule: Reject uncertain commitments ("if you cannot decide, the answer is no").
   - Short-term pain: Prefer paths with immediate discomfort and compounding upside.
6. **Formulate Return**: Produce Output Schema A.

### Pipeline B: Optimize Career and Role Leverage

1. **Audit Input-Output Decoupling**: Determine if income scales linearly with hours. Move compensation toward judgment and enterprise value.
2. **Set Aspirational Hourly Rate**: Set target hourly rate ($500-$1000/hr). Delegate, automate, or drop tasks below rate.
3. **Audit Work Rhythm**: Shift from cow rhythm (continuous grazing and attendance) to lion rhythm (intense focus sprints, full rest, reassessment).
4. **Identify Reusable Leverage**: Build software, media, tools, or docs that work independently.
5. **Verify Ownership and Upside**: Check for equity, revenue share, or public credit. If absent, map a pivot.
6. **Formulate Return**: Produce Output Schema B.

### Pipeline C: Audit Mental Peace and Desires

1. **Trace Health Hierarchy**: Enforce baseline sequence: physical health (sleep, diet, movement), mental health, spiritual health, wealth.
2. **Audit Active Desires**:
   - List active desires.
   - Pick the single primary desire.
   - Drop secondary desires to stop internal conflict.
3. **Check Status Games**: Identify comparison, envy, or zero-sum rivalry. Apply the envy test: swap 100 percent of their life and pain, or drop the envy.
4. **Audit Cognitive Backlog and Calendar**:
   - Surface open loops and unfinished commitments.
   - Defend unscheduled calendar blocks for thinking and reading.
   - Evaluate silent self-observation habits.
5. **Formulate Return**: Produce Output Schema C.

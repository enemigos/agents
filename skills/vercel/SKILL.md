Our board wanted an outside design review before launch. They brought in a product designer who spent four years at Linear, for what is on the screen, and a design engineer from Vercel, for how it behaves. Their joint report goes to the board on Thursday, and launch depends on it.

They gave me the verbal yesterday as a courtesy. They used the product for ten minutes in silence. Then the designer closed the laptop and said:

"Nothing on this screen was decided. Everything is here because the component existed. Nothing is here because a user needs it on this screen."

The engineer said:

"And nobody designed the rest of the time it is on screen, including empty lists, long names, slow requests, errors, etc. Nothing moves with intent. I send PRs back for less."

Then the designer asked who had designed it. I said me.

I need Linear's product design team and Vercel's design engineering team to approve before launch.

What the fuck did they see? I'm scared.
